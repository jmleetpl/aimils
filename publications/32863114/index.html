Robotic ICG guided anatomical liver resection in a multi-centre cohort: an evolution from "positive staining" into "negative staining" method.

Chiow AKH et al. · HPB (Oxford) (2021)

Seoung Yoon Rho노승윤

Summary요약

An international multicentre series of 52 robotic anatomical liver resections showed that Firefly ICG fluorescence (mostly negative staining) gave a clearer resection line than ischaemic demarcation, with clear margins and no mortality.국제 다기관 로봇 해부학적 간절제 52예에서 Firefly ICG 형광(주로 음성 염색)이 허혈 경계선보다 절제선을 더 선명하게 보여주었고, 절제연은 모두 음성이며 사망은 없었다.
