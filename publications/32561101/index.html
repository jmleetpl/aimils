Laparoscopic liver resection versus open liver resection for intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma: 3-year outcomes of a cohort study with propensity score matching.

Kang SH et al. · Surg Oncol (2020)

Woohyung Lee이우형

Summary요약

Propensity-matched comparison for intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma showed laparoscopic resection shortened hospital stay without increasing complications or worsening 3-year survival versus open resection.간내 담관암에서 성향점수 매칭 비교 결과, 복강경 간절제는 개복과 비교해 합병증 증가나 3년 생존 악화 없이 재원기간을 줄였다.
