Clinical Significance of Real-Time Guidance Using the ICG Fluorescent Rim on the Resection Plane During Liver Parenchymal Transection.

Kim JH et al. · World J Surg (2025)

Ji Hoon Kim김지훈

Summary요약

Evaluates a fluorescent ICG rim seen on the liver cut surface during laparoscopic hepatectomy as real-time guidance for tumour margins, supporting its preliminary safety for margin control and parenchymal preservation.복강경 간절제 중 절단면에 보이는 ICG 형광 테두리를 종양 절제연의 실시간 지표로 활용하는 방법을 평가했으며, 절제연 확보와 간실질 보존에 대한 초기 안전성을 확인했다.
